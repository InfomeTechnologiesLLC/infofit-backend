Why people can't cheat

  • Only data the phone, watch or a workout app records by itself counts. Anything typed in by hand in a fitness app is removed.
  • Only each person's one active phone counts; a second phone adds nothing.
  • Everything recorded on the challenge dates counts (whole days, from the start date to the end date), even for people who join later. Nothing before the start date or after the end date counts.

  • Impossible days are capped (50,000 steps, 60 km, 2,500 kcal) and shown to you as unusual days.
